 AI Job Trends & Automation Risk Analytics Dashboard

This Power BI Capstone Project explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the global job landscape.

#Project Overview

The dashboard is divided into two main sections:

1. Executive Overview
- Summarizes AI impact levels by industry
- Displays job openings by year
- Provides salary projections up to 2030

2. Detailed Analytics Dashboard
- Breaks down automation risks and high-risk industries
- Compares global salary averages
- Uses DAX measures for data-driven insights

---

## Key Learnings
- Power BI for data visualization and storytelling  
- DAX for analytical calculations  
- Data cleaning and modeling for accuracy  
- Dashboard design for strategic insights  

---

## Goal
To demonstrate how data analytics can guide AI workforce planning and help organizations anticipate future skill demands.
